- dated source archives or commits showing authorship of first-party files;
- design drafts and asset source files;
- records showing that the public nickname belongs to the maintainer;
- permissions or assignments from every contributor;
- source URLs, downloaded artifacts, hashes, and license snapshots for dependencies;
- the final sensitive-data, trademark, license, and archive review report.
