# Acceptable use and generated content

This project is a general creation tool, not a source of legal, medical,
financial, or safety-critical advice. Operators and users are responsible for
their configured models, inputs, outputs, and publication channels.

Do not use the software to:

- upload or reproduce content, brands, likenesses, datasets, or confidential
  material without the necessary rights and permissions;
- remove provenance marks or imitate a third party in a way that misleads users;
- create fraud, impersonation, harassment, exploitation, illegal sexual content,
  unlawful surveillance, or instructions intended to cause harm;
- make unsubstantiated product, health, performance, price, certification, or
  environmental claims;
- make automated high-impact decisions about people.

Before commercial publication, a human must verify factual claims, image rights,
trademark use, consent, model/provider terms, platform rules, and applicable
advertising law. Clearly label AI-generated or materially altered content and
preserve provenance metadata where required. Regulated products and industries
require separate qualified review; the default repository intentionally includes
no medical-product marketing template.
